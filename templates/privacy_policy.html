At LexRP Advocates & Consultants, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard the information you provide when using our website and submitting contact or inquiry forms.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily submit to us, including your name, telephone/mobile number, email address, address details, and any documents or case details you choose to upload or message through our contact forms.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Understand your legal needs and provide appropriate advisory/representation services.
  • Contact you in response to your callback or consultation booking requests.
  • Assess career applications and CV submissions.
  • Fulfill statutory or regulatory requirements governing advocates.
3. Confidentiality & Security

In accordance with professional standards and advocate ethics, all client information is kept strictly confidential. We implement physical, electronic, and administrative safeguards to secure your personal data against unauthorized access or disclosure.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, except when required by law or with your explicit consent for legal proceedings.

5. Your Choices

You can request to review, edit, or delete your contact details stored with us by sending an email to advrupesh2022@gmail.com.
